How to construct the perfect Seed deck

Sarah Jenkins

Oct 12, 2026 • 5 min read

When you're raising a Seed round, investors are evaluating two things: the magnitude of the problem, and why your specific team is the one to solve it. Everything else is secondary.

The Problem Slide

Don't waste time explaining macro-trends everyone already knows. If you're building in climate tech, we know the world is getting warmer. What we don't know is the specific friction point in carbon accounting that your software eliminates.

Be specific. Be urgent.

The Solution

Show, don't tell. A screenshot or a 10-second GIF of your product in action is worth a thousand bullet points.

Key Actionable Takeaways

  • Keep your pitch deck under 12 slides for initial VC intro calls.
  • Put team credentials and unique insights upfront on slide 2 or 3.
  • Explicitly quantify bottom-up Total Addressable Market (TAM).
