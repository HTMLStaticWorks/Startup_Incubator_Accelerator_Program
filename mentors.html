FAQ

Mentor questions

How the matching works, and what you can expect once you are in the cohort.

How are mentors matched to my startup?

After onboarding we map your industry, stage and current bottlenecks against the mentor bench, then pair you with 3–4 operators who have solved that exact problem before. You can request a re-match at any point in the 12 weeks.

How much time do mentors actually commit?

Every mentor commits to a weekly sprint session for the length of the cohort, plus async access through our shared Slack. Most founders get a reply the same day.

Do mentors take equity in my company?

No. Mentorship is included in the program — mentors are not compensated with your equity and take no board seat. Ventro's only stake is the standard $150K for 7% post-money SAFE.
