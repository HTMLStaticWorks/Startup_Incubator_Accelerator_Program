The Ventro Cohort Program

A 12-week intensive curriculum designed to help you find product-market fit and raise your next round.

Curriculum

Program Phases

Structured milestones to keep you focused on execution.

1
Weeks 1–4

Onboarding & Build

We start with a deep dive into your metrics and product roadmap. The goal is to establish a rigorous weekly sprint cycle, talk to 100 users, and refine your core value proposition.

2
Weeks 5–8

Mentorship & Growth

You'll be paired with 3-4 specialized mentors matching your industry and growth bottlenecks. Focus shifts to scalable acquisition, optimizing unit economics, and executing GTM experiments.

3
Weeks 9–12

Fundraise & Demo Day

Intensive pitch practice, refining your data room, and mock VC meetings. It culminates in Demo Day, where you'll present to an audience of 300+ verified early-stage investors.

Masterclasses

Weekly Masterclasses

No fluff. Tactical 60-minute workshops led by founders who have built $100M+ companies.

Week 2

Cap Table & SAFE Law

Structuring Delaware C-Corps, equity splits, option pools, and avoiding toxic investor term clauses.

Week 5

Pricing & Unit Economics

Optimizing SaaS tiers, value-based pricing, CAC paybacks, and LTV retention loops.

Week 10

Pitch Deck Storytelling

Crafting an unignorable seed deck story and surviving aggressive VC Q&A sessions.
