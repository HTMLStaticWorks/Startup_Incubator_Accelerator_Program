Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Ventro before reaching out.

What equity standard do you take?

We invest $150,000 for a standard 7% post-money SAFE equity stake on simple, founder-friendly terms.

Is the program remote or in-person?

Ventro is hybrid. Weeks 1 and 12 (Demo Day) take place in San Francisco, CA. The remaining 10 weeks are fully remote.

Can I apply if I am solo or pre-revenue?

Yes! Over 40% of our accepted startups join at pre-revenue stage, and we back high-velocity solo founders regularly.

How long does the application review process take?

We review applications on a rolling basis. You will hear back from our investment partners within 14 business days of submission.

What happens after Demo Day?

After presenting to 300+ accredited investors, alumni gain lifelong access to partner perks ($1M+ in AWS/Stripe credits), follow-on syndicate support, and our global mentor network.
